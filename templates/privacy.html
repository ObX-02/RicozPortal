Privacy Policy

This page explains how information provided through RicozPortal is handled and protected.

Last updated: October 1, 2026
01

Information We Collect

When you use RicozPortal, information such as your name, email address, company information, account details, and information submitted through support requests may be stored to provide portal services.

02

How We Use Information

Information associated with your account may be used to provide and maintain portal functionality, manage your account, process service requests, respond to support tickets, and communicate important account updates.

03

Account Security

Account passwords are stored using password hashing rather than being stored as plain text. You should also keep your login credentials private and avoid sharing your password with other people.

04

Support Information

Information that you submit through the support form may be retained so that support requests can be tracked, reviewed, and responded to.

05

Data Protection

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to help protect account information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

06

Third-Party Services

RicozPortal may rely on infrastructure and service providers required to operate the application. Information handled by such providers is subject to the applicable terms and privacy practices of those services.

07

Your Responsibilities

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and for ensuring that information submitted through your account is accurate and appropriate.

08

Policy Updates

This privacy information may be updated from time to time as the portal and its services evolve. Updated information will be reflected on this page.
